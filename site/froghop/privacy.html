Frog Hop — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Frog Hop (the "App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is made by Peter Fam ("we", "us").

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your scores, collected flies and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. These services collect some device and usage data, described below.
  • You can control ad personalization and usage analytics inside the App (Settings) and in your device settings.

Information collected by the services we use

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob (ads) Advertising ID (where permitted), IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions. To show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you consent, personalize ads.
Firebase Analytics (Google) App interactions (e.g. runs played, scores, which hazards end a run), app instance ID, device model, OS version, country/region, session times. To understand how the game is played and improve its difficulty and features. Analytics data is not used to identify you personally.
Firebase Crashlytics (Google) Crash reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, app state at the time of a crash. To find and fix bugs.

These services are provided by Google LLC. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states): when required, the App asks for your consent on first launch using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad: the App asks permission before using your device's advertising identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android: you can reset or delete it in Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device).
  • Usage analytics: turn off Settings → Share usage stats in the App.
  • Your local progress: Settings → Reset progress erases your scores and flies; uninstalling the App removes everything stored on your device.

Data retention

We keep analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, after which they are deleted. Data processed by AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in California and other US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information, and to object to its processing. Because we don't hold accounts or know who you are, most data is linked only to random device identifiers; contact us and we will help with any request, including through Google's tools where the data is held by Google.

Security

Data is sent to Google's services over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use reputable services and collect as little as we can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App gains new features. The effective date above will change, and significant changes will be noted in the App or its store listing.
